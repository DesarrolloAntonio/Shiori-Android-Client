An Android client for <b>Shiori</b>, the self-hosted bookmark manager. It talks to a server you run yourself: there is no account to sign up for and no service behind the app, only your own.

Your whole library is kept on the device, so the list, the tags and every article you have saved are there with no connection at all. What you change while offline (adding, editing, deleting, asking for a fresh copy of a page) is queued and sent when your server can be reached again.

<b>Features</b>

• <b>Save from any app</b>: share a page from your browser and it lands in your library, without opening the app.
• <b>Read offline</b>: articles are stored as clean, readable text, without the site around them.
• <b>Works offline, syncs later</b>: changes made without a connection are sent once the server answers again.
• <b>Search</b> titles, excerpts and URLs on the device, so it also works offline.
• <b>Tags</b>: filter the list by tag, rename or delete tags, or hide one tag from the list.
• <b>Batch actions</b>: select several bookmarks to tag, re-cache or delete them in one go.
• <b>EPUB</b>: download the ebook your server made of a page and open it in your reader app.
• <b>Phones, tablets and foldables</b>: the list takes as many columns as the screen can hold, and on a large screen the article can open beside it.
• <b>Material 3</b>, light and dark.

<b>Requirements</b>

• A Shiori server you can reach, version 1.7 or newer.
• Android 8.0 or newer.
